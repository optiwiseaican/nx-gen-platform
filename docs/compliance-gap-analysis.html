What the lab apps already enforce, and what NxGen still has to own

LabApp-WebSocket-M (pH / EC meters) and TLC-Tablet-App (NxGen TLC Scan 10) each built their own compliance layer on the tablet. The master specification moves that layer into the NxGen platform. This page lists every compliance capability in the two apps and shows where NxGen stands on each. Rev C adds a pH section comparing the NxGen pH module with NxGen Sense (LabApp-WebSocket-M) feature by feature.

Rev C · 9 Nov 2026

What changed since Rev B

  • Module system (commit 2b93100): instrument modules installed as vendor-signed .nxmod packages, verified on every start, with their own audited tables, permissions and workflows.
  • pH module, first version (commit 2b93100): live readings from pH portables through the USB dongle; every packet stored once with SHA-256; calibration taken from the meter and graded against the LabApp limits.
  • TLC module, first version (commit 50a90aa): plate analyses on approved methods and usable instruments; plate images written once with SHA-256; Rf windows and system suitability; Prepared → Reviewed → Approved with signatures covering the image hashes.
  • Per-user session timing (commits 8aefaca, 3a2e1a3): administrators can give one account its own inactivity sign-out and maximum session, within the validated limits, with reason and signature.
  • New pH section: eight pH capabilities of NxGen Sense compared with the NxGen pH module.
  • pH measurements (pH module 1.1.0): stable readings recorded from the instrument packets with sample, batch and AR number; measured values unchangeable; signed review and approval.
Rev B · 7 Nov 2026

What changed since Rev A

  • Compliance core gaps closed (commit 253cdc1): editable security policy, audit trail filters and signed CSV export, session-expiry warning with audited “Stay signed in”, vendor-signed administrator recovery.
  • Workflow engine and method approval (commit 0dcd3b8): prepare, review, approve, reject, withdraw, invalidate and new versions, every step signed with a fixed meaning; records locked in the database once submitted.
  • Instrument register and calibration (commit 28e1cfb): instruments blocked from use when uncalibrated, failed, overdue, out of service or retired; pH calibrations graded against the LabApp limits and approved by an independent QA approver.
  • Backup and restore deferred at your request.

Findings in the apps

Problems to fix or avoid carrying over

  • LabApp diagnostics module deleted. Commit b1d44fe (13 Aug 2026) removed it, yet DIAGNOSTIC_TEST_CASE_COMPLIANCE_AUDIT.md still reports 87 of 87 test cases implemented.
  • TLC hard-deletes unsigned analyses, raw images included. Only the audit entry remains.
  • TLC separate-reviewer rule is off by default, so one person can prepare, review and approve their own work.
  • LabApp EC module has no permission checks and no signatures.
  • LabApp backups are not encrypted, and the mini-PC transfer uses plain TCP.
  • Much work is uncommitted: about 48 files plus new modules in TLC, 33 changes in LabApp.
  • Docs disagree with code in both repositories, for example TLC feature-status.md still lists authentication and audit as not built.
